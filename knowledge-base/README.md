# Knowledge base

`Adrian_Cioanca_Master_Knowledge_Base.md` is the source of truth for everything this repo
produces. It is not a resume and is never sent anywhere: it is the superset that resumes
are trimmed out of, compiled from 20+ prior resume and CV versions, cover letters and
project notes.

## The rule

**No claim reaches a draft unless it is in here, or Adrian has confirmed it.** Adding a
fact to a resume means adding it here first, with its source. A draft is a selection from
this document, never an addition to it.

## Conflicting figures

Numbers that disagree across prior versions are recorded with **all** their variants and a
`⚠ FLAG`, rather than silently resolved. Open at the time of writing:

- postdoc funding
- the ErythroSight raise
- publication count
- the J.G. Crawford Prize cohort size and nominee count
- which email and phone number are the current primary

Reconcile before publishing, never during drafting: pick the best-supported number, be able
to cite it against the in-repo grants table or Google Scholar, or ask Adrian. A resume with
an unreconciled figure does not ship — see `CLAUDE.md`.

## Maintaining it

- Keep the section structure. The builder instructions and `CLAUDE.md` refer to sections
  by name, **Career Intentions** in particular, which states what the career move is for.
- When a variant is resolved, replace it with the settled number and say what settled it.
  Deleting the variants without recording the reconciliation loses the audit trail.
- New evidence — a grant, a publication, a client engagement — goes in as soon as it
  exists, with the number attached. Backfilling from memory is how variants are born.
