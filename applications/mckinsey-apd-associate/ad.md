McKinsey & Company — Associate (Advanced Professional Degree / APD track)

ROLE
Associate — hired via the Advanced Professional Degree (APD) pathway, which
recruits postdocs and candidates completing a PhD, MD, JD or PharmD directly
into the Associate role. APD candidates make up more than a third of each
incoming Associate class. Eligibility: graduated or on track to graduate by
the relevant intake date; where a non-MBA graduate degree is involved, at
least four years between completion of the undergraduate degree and the
graduate degree is required.

WHAT YOU'LL DO
As an Associate, you will join a client service team and take ownership of a
workstream to solve some of the toughest challenges our clients face.
Associates lead their own workstreams, taking ownership of complex problems,
and work side-by-side with clients to uncover root issues, design creative
solutions, and build in others the capabilities to deliver lasting impact.
Working alongside clients and other McKinsey colleagues, you will combine
research, data analysis, interviews and brainstorming to generate fresh
insights and breakthrough solutions.

WHO YOU'LL WORK WITH
Associates join McKinsey's offices and practices around the world to work in
teams and directly with clients. Associates receive structured training plus
frequent coaching and mentoring from colleagues on their teams, and a
Partner from the local office or practice is assigned to help guide your
career.

QUALIFICATIONS
- Bachelor's degree and excellent academic record required; an advanced
  graduate degree (PhD, MD, JD, PharmD, MBA, etc.) is typical for this
  pathway.
- Outstanding record of academic and professional/managerial achievement.
- Exceptional analytical and quantitative problem-solving skills.
- Strong record of leadership in an academic, professional or
  extracurricular setting.
- Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
- Excellent organisational skills, including the ability to initiate tasks
  independently and see them through to completion.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills in English and the local
  office language(s), with the ability to adjust style for different
  audiences and seniority levels.
- Comfortable making rational, well-reasoned decisions based on data, facts
  and logical reasoning.
- Able to produce work-product-focused materials — PowerPoint decks, Excel
  models, written deliverables — under time pressure.

APPLICATION NOTES
McKinsey states it does not require a cover letter for most roles and
programs, though APD/graduate pathways in some regions ask for GPA and test
scores. A cover letter is being prepared regardless, per this repo's
standard application package.
